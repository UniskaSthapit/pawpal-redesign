Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy describes how this PawPal deployment handles data. Organisations deploying PawPal should have it reviewed against the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and add their legal entity details.

What we collect

Your name, email, and — if you provide them — phone number and address; the answers in adoption applications; questions you send to shelters; pets you save; and, if you use PawPal matching, the lifestyle description you write.

How we use it

To run your account, send your application and enquiries to the shelter caring for the pet, notify you of updates by email, SMS (if you verify your phone) and in the app, and to suggest compatible pets.

Who can see it

Your applications and enquiries are visible to you and to staff at the shelter caring for that pet. Suitability scores and staff notes are internal to shelter staff. We never sell your information.

AI features

When AI features are enabled, PawPal sends a language model only what's needed: public pet information, your message, and — when you ask about it — the status of your own applications. Passwords, verification codes, contact details and shelter-internal notes are never sent.

Service providers

Depending on configuration, PawPal uses a database host (MongoDB Atlas), an email provider (e.g. Resend or SMTP), an SMS provider (Twilio) for verification codes, an AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI), Google Maps for the vet finder, and Unsplash for demo photos.

Cookies

PawPal uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It can't be read by page scripts. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Verification and reset links are random, single-use, stored only as hashes and expire. Phone codes expire after 10 minutes and are rate-limited.

Your rights

You can view and update your details in your profile, clear your lifestyle profile at any time, and ask us to access, correct or delete your information via the contact page.
