Terms of use

Last updated 29 September 2026

These terms describe how PawPal works. Organisations deploying PawPal should have it reviewed and add their legal entity details.

Using PawPal

You may use PawPal to browse pets, save favourites, ask shelters questions and apply to adopt, for lawful personal purposes.

Your responsibilities

Give accurate information in your applications — you are responsible for your answers. Keep your login private and don't misuse or try to disrupt the service.

Pet listings

Listings are provided by shelters and may change or be withdrawn. Details are given in good faith; ask the shelter to confirm anything important.

Matching and AI features

Match percentages and assistant answers are guidance based on the information available. They can't predict how an individual animal will behave and are not a recommendation to adopt.

Applications

Submitting an application is not a guarantee of adoption. The shelter caring for the pet makes every decision.

Changes

We may update these terms. Continuing to use PawPal means you accept the updated terms.
