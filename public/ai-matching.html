How PawPal matching works

Honest matches, clearly explained

  • 1. You describe your life

    PawPal picks out the things that matter: space, time, energy, experience and who else lives with you.

  • 2. We check shelter facts

    Every available pet has been assessed by shelter staff — energy, size, whether they need a yard, how they are with kids and other animals.

  • 3. You see why — and what to consider

    Each match shows a compatibility percentage, the reasons it fits and anything worth thinking about.

A match percentage is guidance to help you explore. It can't predict exactly how an animal will behave — the shelter team always makes the final decision with you.
